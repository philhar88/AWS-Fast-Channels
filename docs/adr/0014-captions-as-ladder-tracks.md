# 0014 — Captions are IMSC1 tracks of the ladder, sourced provided, then embedded, then generated

**Status:** Accepted (supersedes [0013](0013-captions-roadmap.md))

## Context
FAST outlets and US IP-captioning rules expect closed captions. Live experiments through MediaConvert, Channel
Assembly and MediaTailor (October 2026) showed:

- MediaConvert's CMAF WebVTT output is one unsegmented file per track. Channel Assembly then finds no common segment
  boundary, drops every ad break, and refuses the DASH manifest. Its IMSC1 output is segmented on the video grid and
  serves HLS and DASH.
- CEA-608/708 cannot be embedded in CMAF video by MediaConvert, and AV1 can never carry it.
- Channel Assembly matches subtitle renditions by position. A program or slate with different tracks than the one
  before it drops the ad break without an alert, answers 404 for the subtitle playlist, or resets sequence numbers.
- MediaConvert Probe sees only track-based captions (QuickTime `c608`); captions in video SEI, MPEG-2 user data or
  MXF VANC need a job to find them. An `EMBEDDED` selector that finds nothing completes with empty output.

## Decision
A ladder declares its caption tracks (`[[captions]]`: language, label, kind). Every encode with it writes one IMSC1
output per track in the CMAF group, listed in every rendition profile manifest; slates, house ads and uncaptioned
titles get empty tracks (`NULL_SOURCE`). Tracks are part of the ladder fingerprint (a ladder without captions keeps
its earlier version), and the ladder signature that Finalize and VOD-source registration check includes them.

Each track's source is resolved per asset: a provided file (request or drop-folder sibling), else, for the spoken
language, captions in the source (a Probe-visible `c608` track, or a caption scan job), else generated by the
stage's captioner ([0015](0015-pluggable-captioner-elemental-inference.md)), else an empty track. One stage setting
(`captions.enabled`, `required`, `engine`) controls it all; requests can override the mode per asset.

## Consequences
Turning captions on is a new ladder version: its kit is re-provisioned, and titles must be re-encoded to air on it.
Outlets that require 608/708 need an extra TS output (not included). Players must render IMSC1 (hls.js, AVPlayer,
ExoPlayer, dash.js and Shaka do; some TV stacks need WebVTT, an extension point). During SSAI ads MediaTailor puts a
blank WebVTT into every subtitle rendition. A caption scan costs one small AVC and one AAC output per asset that has
no provided captions.
